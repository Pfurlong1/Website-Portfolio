Procedural generating Cave Systems

This project was created as part of my masters thesis which consists of three different algorithms including Open Simplex Noise, Cellular Automata and Marching Cubes developed using C++ and implemented inside of Unreal Engine 5.4.4, in order to conduct a comparative analysis between the three in order to observe different traits, such as but not limited to: visual quality, ease of implementation, optimisation and the believability of the environments.
